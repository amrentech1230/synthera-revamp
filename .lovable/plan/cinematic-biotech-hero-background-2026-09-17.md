# Cinematic Biotech Hero Background

## Goal
Replace the homepage hero’s current abstract/3D backdrop with a premium photorealistic biotechnology background tailored for readable marketing copy.

## Changes
- Generate a wide 16:9 background with a glowing DNA helix concentrated on the right and clean dark space on the left.
- Use deep black and midnight navy with restrained cyan, turquoise, volumetric light, particles, molecular detail, fog, and depth.
- Exclude all text, logos, people, products, symbols, charts, and interface elements from the image.
- Integrate the new asset behind the existing homepage headline and calls to action.
- Remove competing hero visuals so the generated DNA scene remains the focal point.
- Preserve dark overlays for text contrast and tune the crop for desktop and mobile.

## Validation
- Check the homepage at desktop and mobile sizes for image coverage, readable text, balanced DNA placement, and no overlap.
- Confirm the page loads without browser errors.
